# CV-Referral-Candidate-Shortlisting-System

## Overview

The CV Referral & Candidate Shortlisting System is a smart recruitment platform designed to automate the candidate screening and referral process. The system uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze job descriptions, parse resumes, evaluate candidate qualifications, rank applicants, and automate recruitment communications.

The goal of this project is to reduce manual effort in recruitment, improve hiring accuracy, and accelerate the candidate selection process.

---

# Key Features

## Job Posting Management

* Create and manage job vacancies.
* Upload job descriptions in PDF, DOCX, or TXT formats.
* Automatically extract:

  * Required skills
  * Qualifications
  * Certifications
  * Experience requirements
  * Keywords

---

## Bulk CV Upload

* Upload multiple CVs simultaneously.

# System Workflow

1. Recruiter creates or uploads a job posting.
2. AI extracts job requirements.
3. Recruiter uploads candidate CVs in bulk.
4. AI parses candidate information.
5. AI evaluates and scores each applicant.
6. Candidates are ranked automatically.
7. Recruiter reviews shortlisted candidates.
8. Automated emails are sent.
9. Interviews are scheduled.
10. Hiring analytics are updated.
